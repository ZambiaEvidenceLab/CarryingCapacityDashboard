# Exclude outcome indicators from Sector Index math

Sectors like Health have candidate outcome indicators (e.g. under-15 mortality) alongside capacity indicators. We decided to keep Outcome Indicators out of the Sector Index calculation entirely, treating them only as future candidates for correlation analysis against the finished index (per OECD Handbook Step 9 — "Links to Other Variables"), not as index inputs. A Sector Index measures *capacity to act*; outcome indicators measure *results*, which are confounded by factors no ministry directly controls. Mixing the two would turn a capacity-assessment tool into a performance scorecard, which is a different (and contested) use case from the one MoFNP asked for.
