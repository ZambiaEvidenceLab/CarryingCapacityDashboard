# No composite Carrying Capacity score across sectors

We decided the five Sector Indices (Health, Education, Agriculture, Infrastructure, Environment) will never be combined into one overall district score — they stay separate, always shown as five axes on a radar chart, not averaged into a single ranking. The sectors are not commensurate: government capacity in health is not fungible with capacity in agriculture. A single composite would also produce misleading headlines for structurally different districts — e.g. an urban district like Lusaka scoring low on Agriculture for entirely expected reasons would drag down an overall number in a way that invites the wrong policy read.
